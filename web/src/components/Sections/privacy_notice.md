# DibraSpeaks Privacy Notice

**Effective date:** Friday, 19 December 2025  
**Last updated:** Friday, 19 December 2025

This Privacy Notice explains how the operator of the hosted DibraSpeaks instance you are using (“**we**,” “**us**,” “**our**”) collects, uses, shares, and stores information when you use **DibraSpeaks** (the “**Service**”), including our website, web app, APIs, and dataset download pages.

## Key points (read this first)
- **Your voice and text contributions may become public.** If you record audio, upload audio, submit sentences, write transcripts, or validate clips, your contributions may be released in public datasets that anyone can download and use.
- **Public datasets can spread.** Once a dataset is public, we cannot fully control copies, mirrors, re-uploads, or downstream uses (including AI model training).
- **Do not submit personal or sensitive information.** Please avoid speaking or typing names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, IDs, medical info, or any confidential content.
- **You can request removal from future releases.** We can often remove your contributions from our active database and future dataset releases, but we may not be able to remove data already downloaded by others.

If you do not agree with the public nature of contributions, please do not contribute.
